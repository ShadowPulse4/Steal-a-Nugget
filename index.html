Chapter 01

The basics

Steal a Nugget is a Roblox heist game about fast food. Run down a long lane of 30 biomes, grab raw nuggets off the counters of the fast food workers, outrun them back to the safe zone and fry what you stole. Only the fryer shows what you really got: from a plain Common Nugget to a Godly one.

Give your fried nuggets to the customers on your Plot and they pay you money every second. Train your speed on the treadmill to steal from faster biomes, hatch pets, chase Mutation Rains and fight the two bosses that take turns every 4 hours.

Chapter 02

Controls

A handful of keys is all you need.

  • EholdSteal a nugget, use machines (Egg Spinner, Fuse Machine), give a nugget to a customer.
  • ClickPut a nugget in a fryer on your Plot, place a Trap, swing the Spatula.
  • Q/DROPDrop your stolen nugget. While a worker is chasing you, that takes 3 seconds.
  • GOpen the inventory: Pets, Nuggets and Gear tabs, with search. The backpack button at the top of the screen does the same.
  • 1–0Hotbar slots 1 to 0.
  • JumpStop running on the treadmill.

On the right of the screen you'll find the Pets, Fryer, Nuggets, Music and Invite buttons. The Shop and the Index are on the left.

The Night

Field closed · 12

Every 5 minutes night falls. Everyone in the field is sent back to their Plot, every counter gets a new nugget, and a dark barrier keeps the field closed for 12 seconds, with a countdown.

Good to know

A stolen nugget is gone from its counter until the next Night refills the field.

Chapter 04

Escaping the workers

Grab a nugget and the workers of that biome see you (“!”) and come after you. They chase you through the whole field until they catch you or you reach the safe zone.

Safe zone
  • Mystery loot. On the counters every raw nugget looks the same and is the same size. You find out its rarity when it comes out of the fryer. Special nuggets are the exception: you can recognise them.
  • True size. Once you steal it, a nugget shows its true size: the rarer, the bigger it is above your head. A Godly one is 2.5x as big as a Common one. Dropped on the ground it keeps that size, so you, and everyone who wants to steal it from you, can guess how good it is. It shrinks into the basket when you fry it.
  • Hands full. In the field you hold the nugget above your head and can't put it away.
  • Caught? You're knocked down as a ragdoll for 3 seconds, and the worker carries the nugget back to its counter.
  • Made it? Once you're over the red line in the safe zone, the nugget is yours for good. Walk into the field with it later and the workers leave you alone.
  • Let go. Press Q or the DROP button to drop your stolen nugget. While a worker is chasing you, that takes 3 seconds.

Carrying slows you down

Carrying a raw nugget slows you down a little. It's the same for every raw nugget. Carry pets make it less:

Tip

Getting caught in a new biome? Its workers are faster than you. Train on your treadmill and try again.

Chapter 05

Spatula & traps

Out in the field, other players can steal your nugget too. In the safe zone nobody can hit you.

Safe zoneNo PvP
PvP on

The Spatula

Hit another player who's carrying a stolen nugget: they're knocked down as a ragdoll and lie still for 3 seconds, and you can steal their nugget. No hitting in the safe zone. In a boss battle, the spatula is your weapon against the boss.

Bear Traps

You have 3 Bear Traps (6 with the Extra Traps pass). Click a spot in the field, up to 40 studs away, to place one. Not in the safe zone, and 3 seconds between traps.

  1. SnapAnother player carrying a stolen nugget steps in and drops it. Anyone can grab it.
  2. StuckThey're stuck for 7 seconds, with a countdown everyone can see.
  3. Back to youThe trap opens and goes back to your hotbar. The freed player can't be trapped again for 3 seconds.
Tip

On your way back with a nugget, watch the floor between you and the red line.

Chapter 06

The fryer

Click a spot on your Plot to put a stolen nugget in a fryer. When it's done, take it out: now you see its rarity, and its mutation if it has one.

  • 8fryers at once
  • 30 s → 24 hThe rarer the nugget, the longer it fries: 30 seconds for a Common, 24 hours for a Prismatic.
  • OfflineFrying goes on while you're offline.
  • 60nuggets fit in your bag

Taking a nugget out of the fryer brings a hungry customer to your Plot. More on them in Plot & customers.

Fry faster

  • x2 Frying pass. Everything fries twice as fast.
  • Fry pets. Up to 60% faster: Guinea Pig 8%, Bear 18%, Dragon 30%, Lava Golem 45%.
  • Fry All. Fry everything in your fryers at once with the button in the Fryer menu. It costs 199 R$ every time you use it.

Chapter 09

Rains & mutations

Every 10 minutes a Mutation Rain falls on one biome for 30 seconds, with a storm sky and its own music. The timer already shows which rain is next and where.

  • The strike. 3–7 seconds after the rain starts, it strikes one nugget in that biome and gives it the rain's mutation. Every rain strikes in its own way: lightning, a giant candy, an icicle, goo, a meteor, a chrome ball, a diamond, a dark portal, a shooting star.
  • While it rains. New nuggets in that biome can get the mutation too, at 5x the normal chance (higher in the far biomes). Outside the rains, nuggets never mutate.
  • On top of the rarity. A mutation multiplies the income. A Legendary Candy Nugget pays 6x a Legendary.
  • Bonus for everyone. Every customer eating a mutated nugget also makes all your customers pay +10%.
  • Mutation pets. Frog, Monkey and Hawk can give the rain's mutation to a nugget you fry during a rain.

Chapter 10

Plot & customers

Your Plot is your base in the hub (the safe zone), with your fryers and your treadmill in front of it.

$0per second keeps adding up

Taking a nugget out of the fryer brings a hungry customer to your Plot (there are 10 different looks). Give it a fried nugget and it walks around, eats it and pays you every second. The rarer the nugget, the taller the customer: 6 studs for a Common one, a 100-stud giant for a Prismatic one.

You can take a nugget back from a customer or swap it for a better one.

Plot upgrades

Click the sign on your Plot to upgrade it. You start with room for 6 customers and end with 50 at the last level (up to 55 with Extra Nugget Slots from the Shop). Level 2 costs $1M, level 3 $75M, then every level costs 1.35x more (the last one about $22.3T). Every upgrade makes your Plot bigger and gives its fence a new look:

Money while you're away

Your customers keep paying while you're offline, up to 24 hours. When you come back it waits on your Plot in a glowing circle: walk through it to claim it.

Daily Chest

Once every 24 hours, the Daily Chest in the hub gives you a random $1K–$500K.

The Pets menu

Open it with the Pets button (the paw) on the right.

  • Tabs. All, and one per pet rarity, each with a paw in its color. A rarity you have no pets of is faded.
  • Equip Best / Unequip All. Puts your best pets out, or takes them all back.
  • Search. Type a pet's name; the grid icon shows all your pets again. At the top you see how many pets are out (like “2/3 OUT”) and how many you own (like “44/250”).
  • Cards. Every pet has a card in its rarity's color. A pet that's out has a blue tick. The same pets with the same look share one card (“x4”). A Shiny, Golden, Diamond or Rainbow pet has its icon in the corner.
  • Details. Click a card and the pet turns in a glow on the right, with its name, rarity, look and boosts, an Equip / Unequip button and Delete (press twice: “Sure?”).
  • Total. At the bottom you see what all the pets that are out give you together.

The Index menu

Open it with the INDEX button on the left.

  • Tabs. ALL, and one per page with how many of its nuggets you found (like “7/10”). A red “!” means its reward is waiting; a tick means you claimed it.
  • Rewards. At the top: COLLECTED with how many you found and a bar. A complete page shows CLAIM!, then CLAIMED. On ALL, a gold button shows how many rewards are waiting and takes you to the first one.

How a battle works

  • Portal opens. When the battle starts, the portal in the hub opens for 60 seconds. You need 1M Speed to go in. Walk through it and you come out of the portal in the boss's arena.
  • Doors shut. Once the boss's intro film starts, the portal closes: nobody can join anymore, and nobody can leave until the battle ends.
  • Fight. In the arena everyone runs at the same speed. Hit the boss with your Spatula: 12 damage a hit, 15% chance on a critical hit (double), and double damage while he's dizzy.
  • 8 minutes. The boss has more health for every extra player in the arena. Below half his health he becomes ENRAGED: faster, stronger, and his attacks come sooner.
  • Knocked out? You're out until the next Boss Battle.
  • He falls. He goes up in a big poof and his nugget flies to every player still in the arena. It lands in your bag already fried. That nugget is only available this way.
  • Time's up. The boss wins, laughs at you and everyone goes back to the hub.

The battle has its own music, a health bar with the time left, a Top Damage list, your own health bar, and speech bubbles that type themselves with a typing sound.

Chapter 16

Tips from the back office

  • Fry everything. Every nugget you get home brings a customer, and even a Common keeps paying while you're offline.
  • Check the biome first. Look at the workers' speed of a biome before you steal there. Faster than them? Go.
  • Mind the Night. Every 5 minutes the field resets and every counter gets a new nugget.
  • Chase the rain. The timer shows which rain is next and where. Be in that biome when it strikes.
  • Stack mutations. Every customer eating a mutated nugget makes all your customers pay +10%.
  • Hunt the specials. A Golden Nugget shows up every 4–8 minutes, a Prismatic every 15–25. They don't stay long.
  • Read the size. A big nugget over someone's head is a good one: the rarer, the bigger.
  • Keep room in your bag. A boss nugget lands in your bag already fried. With 60 nuggets in it, there's no room.
  • Keep your duplicates. 2 of the same pet with the same look fuse into the next look.
  • Be ready for the boss. You need 1M Speed, and the portal only stays open for 60 seconds.
  • Claim your offline money. Walk through the glowing circle on your Plot when you come back.
  • Open the Daily Chest. Once every 24 hours, free money in the hub.
Chapter 17

Frequently asked

What happens if a worker catches me?

You're knocked down as a ragdoll for 3 seconds, and the worker carries the nugget back to its counter.

Is my nugget safe in the safe zone?

Yes. Once you're over the red line it's yours for good, and if you walk into the field with it later, the workers leave you alone.

Does a rarer nugget slow me down more?

No. Every raw nugget slows you down the same. Carry pets make it less.

Can I tell how good a nugget is before frying?

A bit. On the counters they all look the same, but once you steal one it shows its true size above your head: the rarer, the bigger (a Godly one is 2.5x a Common one). Its rarity shows when it comes out of the fryer.

Can other players steal my nugget?

Yes, but only out in the field. Get hit by a Spatula while you carry a stolen nugget and you lie still for 3 seconds while they steal it. Step into someone's Bear Trap and you drop it (anyone can grab it) and you're stuck for 7 seconds. Neither works in the safe zone.

Which rarities can I find?

That depends on the biome. Every biome has its own most common nuggets, from Common in the Forest to Godly in Galaxy and Heaven.

How long does frying take?

From 30 seconds for a Common to 24 hours for a Prismatic. Frying goes on while you're offline, and you can use up to 8 fryers at once.

How do mutations work?

Every 10 minutes a Mutation Rain hits one biome for 30 seconds. A mutation multiplies a nugget's income, from x5 (Electric) to x50 (Cosmic).

Do I earn money while I'm offline?

Yes, for up to 24 hours. The money waits on your Plot in a glowing circle.

How do I get faster?

Train on your treadmill, buy better treadmills, and get Speed pets, Index rewards or Speed packs.

Where do I get pets?

From the Egg Spinner in the hub. Hold E at it and spin 1 or up to 10 eggs at once.

How does fusing work?

Put 2 of the same pet with the same look in the Fuse Machine. You get 1 with the next look: Shiny, Golden, Diamond, then Rainbow.

When are the boss battles?

Nugget King at 00:00, 08:00 and 16:00 UTC, Lava Dragon at 04:00, 12:00 and 20:00 UTC.

What do I need for a boss battle?

1M Speed, and you have to walk through the portal in the 60 seconds it's open.

How do I get a King or Dragon Nugget?

Only by beating that boss. When he falls, his nugget flies to every player still in the arena.
