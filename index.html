Chapter 01

The basics

Steal a Nugget is a heist game about fast food. You run down a long lane of counters, grab raw nuggets, outrun the fast food workers guarding them and fry what you stole to find out its rarity.

Every fried nugget you hand to the NPC on your plot earns you money every second. That money, plus the speed you train, lets you push through all 30 biomes, catch rarer nuggets and take on the bosses. The whole game is one loop that keeps getting bigger.

Chapter 03

Escaping the staff

Workers run after you to take the nugget back. If they catch you, it's gone and the run is over.

Safe zone

Your only job now is to reach the safe zone. Once you're inside, the chase is over and the nugget is yours.

Whether you make it comes down to speed. Every biome has its own worker speed. If you're not faster than its workers, they will catch up. See Speed & biomes for how to get faster.

Watch out for other players too. Outside the safe zone they can take your nugget with a spatula or set traps on your way back. See Spatulas & traps.

Heavier loot, slower legs

As soon as you pick up a nugget, you run slower. How much slower depends on the nugget's rarity: the rarer the nugget, the slower you are. So the best nuggets are also the hardest to get away with.

Hint

Running slower than usual on the way back? You might be holding something rare. Don't get caught now.

Tip

Got caught in a new biome? Its workers are probably faster than you, especially with a heavy nugget. Spend a few minutes on the treadmill and try again.

Chapter 04

Spatulas & traps

The staff aren't the only ones after your nugget. Other players can steal it too, but only outside the safe zone.

Safe zoneNo PvP
PvP on

The spatula

Use the spatula on another player to take the nugget they're carrying. It only works outside the safe zone, so a player who made it inside is out of reach.

Traps

Put traps down on the lane for other players to run into on their way back. You can only place them outside the safe zone.

Tip

A rare nugget slows you down, which makes you an easy target. Keep an eye out for traps and players with a spatula, and head straight for the safe zone.

Chapter 05

The fryer

In the safe zone you drop your raw nugget into the fryer. When it comes out, you find out which rarity you actually stole.

  1. Bring it inCarry the raw nugget into the safe zone.
  2. Drop it inPut it in the fryer and let the oil do its work.
  3. See the resultThe fried nugget shows its rarity, and a mutation if it got one.

The rarity is random and stays hidden until the nugget comes out of the fryer. Most of the time you'll get something common, but every fry has a chance at something rare. That's why it's worth frying every nugget you get away with.

Want to feel it? Try the demo fryer further down this page.

Chapter 07

Mutations & Rains

Every now and then a Rain starts. It hits one biome, and nuggets from that biome can come out with a mutation on top of their rarity.

A mutation doesn't replace the rarity, it stacks with it. So a nugget can be Epic and Frost, or Prismatic and Lava. Each Rain brings its own mutation:

  • ElectricElectric Rain
  • CandyCandy Rain
  • FrostFrost Rain
  • ToxicToxic Rain
  • LavaLava Rain
  • ChromeChrome Rain
  • VoidVoid Rain
  • CosmicCosmic Rain
Tip

When a Rain starts, head to the biome it's hitting. Only nuggets from that biome can pick up its mutation.

See every Rain in action in the weather report further down.

Chapter 08

Your plot & income

Fried nuggets are worth nothing in your pocket. Give them to the NPC on your plot and they start paying you money every second.

$0per second keeps adding up

How much a nugget pays depends on its rarity. A Common pays a little, a Prismatic pays a lot. Since the NPC pays every second, even low tiers add up while you're out on your next run.

Your plot is also where you'll find the treadmill, right in front. More on that in the next chapter.

Chapter 09

Speed & biomes

The lane runs through 30 biomes, and each one has its own worker speed. To get your nugget back to the safe zone, you need to be faster than the workers there.

  1. Biome 1
  2. Biome 2
  3. Biome 3
  4. … Biome 30

You build speed on the treadmill in front of your plot. The more you train, the faster you run, and the more biomes you can steal from. Harder biomes need more speed, so training is how you progress.

Don't train just to match the workers. Holding a nugget slows you down, and rarer nuggets slow you down more, so you want speed to spare for the run back.

Tip

Some pets give you extra speed. A speed pet helps you outrun faster workers sooner.

Chapter 12

Tips from the back office

  • Fry everything. Every nugget you escape with is a chance at a high tier, and even a Common keeps paying on your plot.
  • Train before you travel. Be faster than a biome's workers, with room to spare. A rare nugget slows you down on the way back.
  • Plan your exit. Look at the route to the safe zone before you grab a nugget, and watch for traps on the lane.
  • Chase the Rain. When a Rain starts, go to that biome for a shot at a mutation.
  • Keep your duplicates. Two of the same pet means a fuse waiting to happen.
  • Watch the clock. Bosses swap every 4 hours. Know which one is up so you get the reward you want.
Chapter 13

Frequently asked

What happens if a worker catches me?

They take the nugget back. Run back to the counters and grab another one.

Can other players steal my nugget?

Yes, with a spatula, but only outside the safe zone. Once you're in the safe zone, it's yours.

Where can I place traps?

On the lane, outside the safe zone. You can't put traps down inside it.

Why am I slower when I'm holding a nugget?

Carrying a nugget slows you down. The rarer the nugget, the slower you run.

Where do I fry my nuggets?

In the fryer in the safe zone. That's also where the chase ends.

Can I choose which rarity I get?

No. Every nugget's rarity is random, and you only see it once it comes out of the fryer.

Can a nugget have a rarity and a mutation?

Yes. A mutation comes on top of the rarity, so any rarity can also be mutated.

How do I get a mutation?

Steal from the biome a Rain is hitting while it's happening. Each Rain gives its own mutation, like Lava Rain for Lava.

How do I make more money?

Give more and rarer nuggets to the NPC on your plot. Income pets help too.

How many biomes are there?

30. Each one has its own worker speed you need to beat.

How do I get into harder biomes?

Train on the treadmill in front of your plot until you're faster than that biome's workers.

Where do I get pets?

You buy them from the Egg Spinner. Hold E at the machine to spin an egg.

Do I need a team for boss battles?

No. You can fight a boss alone or together with other players.
