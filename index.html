The heist

Four steps from raw to rich

Every run follows the same loop. The faster you get, the further you get through all 30 biomes.

  1. 1

    Steal

    Run down the lane and grab a raw nugget off one of the counters.

    • RFC
    • R
    • Nugget King
  2. 2

    Run

    The staff chase you to take it back, and the nugget slows you down. The rarer it is, the slower you run. Reach the safe zone.

    SAFE
  3. 3

    Fry

    Drop it in the fryer. The oil decides which rarity you walked out with.

  4. 4

    Earn

    Give it to the NPC on your plot. Higher rarity means more money every second.

    $0/s

Weather report

When it rains, nuggets mutate

A Rain hits one biome at a time. Any nugget caught in it can come out with a mutation on top of its rarity.

Forecast: Lava Rain

Lava Mutation

Molten drops fall from a red sky. Nuggets come out cracked and glowing like they never stopped cooking.

  • WhereOne biome per Rain
  • Stacks withAny rarity

Plot gym

Too slow? Hit the treadmill

The lane runs through 30 biomes, and every biome has its own worker speed. If you're not faster than its workers, they catch you before you make it back to the safe zone. And a nugget in your hands slows you down, more the rarer it is. Train on the treadmill in front of your plot for speed to spare.
